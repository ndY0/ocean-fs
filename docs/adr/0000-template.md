## Decision

What did we decide? Be specific. Include:

- The chosen approach (with enough detail that someone reading this
  6 months later can understand what was built).
- Concrete choices: libraries, algorithms, data structures, protocols,
  configuration knobs.
- The scope of the decision — what is in and out of scope.

> This section should describe the outcome, not the deliberation.
> The deliberation lives in Alternatives.
